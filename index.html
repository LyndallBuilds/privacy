Privacy & Terms

Virtues Practice is a private record of how you are living a set of virtues. An account keeps a cloud copy so the practice can follow you to another device.

Your practice is saved on this iPhone first.

If you are signed in, a copy can be stored with your account. The copy includes your virtues, daily check-ins, written reflections, weekly aims, Sunday reviews, and practice settings. It is used only to keep your practice available to you.

That information is linked to your account. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to track you across other companies’ apps.

Your account uses your email address and a password, and a name if you add one. Sign-in is provided by Clerk. Face ID, reminders, and the lock stay on this phone and are not uploaded.

You are responsible for what you write. Deleting your account removes the sign-in and the cloud copy. The copy on this iPhone can be erased at the same time.
